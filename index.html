Grid: 12×12 (144 cells) • Squirrels: 30 (21% of board)

How to Play

Desktop Controls

Left-click to reveal a cell

Right-click to place a flag

Mobile/Tablet Controls

Tap (quick) to reveal a cell

Long press (hold 0.5 seconds) to place a flag

You'll see the cell highlight while holding

Numbers show how many squirrels are nearby. Reveal all safe cells to win!

Features

Chord opening: When enabled, left-click a number with all surrounding squirrels flagged to auto-reveal safe cells around it.

Cell size: Use the slider to adjust board size (50% = tiny, 100% = normal, 200% = huge).

Difficulty Levels

Easy: 8×8 grid with 10 squirrels

Medium: 12×12 grid with 30 squirrels

Hard: 16×16 grid with 40 squirrels

Custom: Create your own board size and squirrel count (up to 50×50)

Performance Tips

• For best performance, keep grids under 30×30 (900 cells)

• Large grids (30×30+) automatically disable animations for smoother gameplay

• Maximum grid size is 50×50 (2,500 cells) with squirrels capped at 30% of the board
